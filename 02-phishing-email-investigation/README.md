# Phishing Email Investigation

## Objective

Investigate a simulated phishing email to identify malicious indicators, determine the risk to the recipient, and recommend appropriate SOC response actions.

## Investigation Scope

The investigation will examine:

- Sender address
- Sender domain
- Recipient
- Email subject
- URLs
- Attachments
- Indicators of compromise (IOCs)
- User interaction
- Recommended containment and remediation

## Investigation Status

Investigation in progress.

Evidence and final analyst conclusions will be added after completing the investigation.

## Tools

- Microsoft Defender
- Microsoft Sentinel
- KQL
- Threat Intelligence
- Email Security Analysis
